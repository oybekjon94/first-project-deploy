The Problem

Students preparing for international exams often face high anxiety and lack a realistic environment to practice. Traditional study methods fail to simulate the actual pressure and format of global language tests, leading to lower confidence and unpredictable results.

Discovery

Through interviews with 20+ IELTS and TOEFL candidates, we identified that the biggest gap is not just knowledge, but time management and familiarity with the digital testing interface.

The Solution

Mockchi bola: Bridging the Gap with Realistic Simulations & Smart Feedback

Judgment-Free Practice

Realistic Mock Tests: AI-powered evaluation providing instant, non-judgmental feedback on speaking and writing tasks.

Personalized Study Paths

Smart Curriculum: Automatically generates a custom study plan based on your mock test performance and weak areas.

Deep Progress Analytics

Data Insights: Track your improvement over time with detailed charts and section-by-section scoring.

Community Insights

Peer Benchmarking: Compare your results with thousands of other candidates to see where you stand globally.
